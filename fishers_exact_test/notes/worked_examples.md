# Example: Treatment vs Control (Small n)
|          | Success | Failure |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Treatment|   4     |    1    |
| Control  |   1     |    4    |

- Compute exact two-tailed p-value ≈ 0.047.
- OR = (4·4)/(1·1) = 16.
- Conclusion: association significant at α = 0.05.
