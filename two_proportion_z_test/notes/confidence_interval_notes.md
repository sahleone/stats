# Formula
$$ (\hat{p}_1 - \hat{p}_2) \pm z_{\alpha/2} \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}_1(1 - \hat{p}_1)}{n_1} + \frac{\hat{p}_2(1 - \hat{p}_2)}{n_2}} $$

# Example
Campaign response: difference = 0.1625, SE = √(0.60·0.40/150 + 0.4375·0.5625/160) ⇒ 95% CI ≈ (0.05, 0.25).

# Interpretation
Since 0 is outside interval, the difference is significant at α = 0.05.
