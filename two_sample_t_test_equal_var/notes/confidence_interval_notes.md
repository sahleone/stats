# Formula
$$ (\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2) \pm t_{\alpha/2, df} \cdot s_p \sqrt{\frac{1}{n_1} + \frac{1}{n_2}} $$
where \( s_p \) is the pooled standard deviation and df = n₁ + n₂ − 2.

# Example
Campaign data: x̄₁ − x̄₂ = 4; sₚ = 5.62; n₁=18, n₂=20; 95% CI ≈ 4 ± 2.028·5.62·√(1/18 + 1/20) ⇒ (1.0, 6.0).

# Interpretation
If 0 is outside the interval, the two-tailed test at α = 0.05 would reject H₀, indicating a significant difference between means.
