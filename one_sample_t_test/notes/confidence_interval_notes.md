# Formula
$$ \bar{x} \pm t_{\alpha/2, df} \cdot \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} $$ where df = n − 1.

# Example
Quiz sample: x̄ = 81.6, s = 6.3, n = 12, 95% CI uses t_{0.025,11} = 2.201 ⇒ 81.6 ± 2.201·6.3/√12 ⇒ (77.6, 85.6).

# Interpretation
“We are 95% confident that the true mean score lies between 77.6 and 85.6.” Since 85 is within, the two-tailed test at α = 0.05 fails to reject H₀.
