# Example 1: Study Habit vs Grade
2×3 table (n = 180). Compute expected counts, χ² ≈ 9.6, df = 2 ⇒ reject H₀ at α = 0.05; active study associated with higher grades.

# Example 2: Device Type vs Purchase
3×2 table (n = 150). χ² ≈ 4.1, df = 2 ⇒ fail to reject at α = 0.05; insufficient evidence of association.
