# Problems
1. 58 of 90 respondents favor a policy. Test H₀: p = 0.60 vs H₁: p ≠ 0.60 at α = 0.05.
2. 12 of 250 shipments were late. Test H₀: p = 0.05 vs H₁: p < 0.05 at α = 0.01.
3. 140 of 220 customers rate service “excellent.” Test α = 0.10 if p > 0.60.
4. In quality control, 22 of 300 parts fail. Test α = 0.05 two-tailed for p = 0.06.

# Solutions
1. p̂=0.644; z≈0.92 ⇒ fail to reject.
2. p̂=0.048; z≈−0.22 ⇒ fail to reject (insufficient evidence at 0.01).
3. p̂=0.636; z≈1.42 ⇒ p≈0.078 <0.10 ⇒ reject (proportion higher).
4. p̂=0.073; z≈1.48 ⇒ p≈0.14 ⇒ fail to reject.
