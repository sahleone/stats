# Confidence Interval?
The chi-square goodness-of-fit test does not map directly to a confidence interval for categorical distributions. Instead, report standardized residuals or category-wise proportion CIs.

# Interpretation Tips
- Highlight categories with |O − E|/√E larger than ~2.
- Discuss practical implications (e.g., color ratios not uniform).
